"Location Not Available" means that the Honor X9b device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Honor X9b device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Honor X9b device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Honor X9b device is not currently sharing its location.
