## Part 1: What Does "Location Not Available" Mean on Find My Friends?

When the "Location Not Available" message appears on Find My Friends, it means that you have disabled location sharing or have turned off your device's location services. In some cases, the message can indicate that you have logged out of your iCloud account or that there is an issue with the iCloud server.

**_Does "Location Not Available" mean that there is something wrong with Infinix Note 30i ?_**

Not! This message does not necessarily mean that you have blocked yourself or there is something wrong with your device. [**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Infinix Note 30i device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Infinix Note 30i device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Infinix Note 30i device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Infinix Note 30i device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Infinix Note 30i device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Infinix Note 30i device is not currently sharing its location.
