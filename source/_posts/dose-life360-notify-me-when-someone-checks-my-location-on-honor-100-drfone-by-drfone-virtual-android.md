[**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Honor 100 device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Honor 100 device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Honor 100 device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Honor 100 device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.
