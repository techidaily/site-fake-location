Not! This message does not necessarily mean that you have blocked yourself or there is something wrong with your device. "Location Not Available" means that the Honor 100 device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Honor 100 device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Honor 100 device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Honor 100 device is not currently sharing its location.
