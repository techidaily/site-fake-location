[**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Samsung Galaxy S23 device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Samsung Galaxy S23 device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Samsung Galaxy S23 device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Samsung Galaxy S23 device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Samsung Galaxy S23 device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Samsung Galaxy S23 device is not currently sharing its location.
