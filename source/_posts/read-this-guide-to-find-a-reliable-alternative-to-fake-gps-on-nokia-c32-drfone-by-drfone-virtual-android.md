When the "Location Not Available" message appears on Find My Friends, it means that you have disabled location sharing or have turned off your device's location services. In some cases, the message can indicate that you have logged out of your iCloud account or that there is an issue with the iCloud server.

**_Does "Location Not Available" mean that there is something wrong with Nokia C32 ?_**

Not! This message does not necessarily mean that you have blocked yourself or there is something wrong with your device. [**_How to distinguish "No Location Found" and "Location Not Available"?_**](https://drfone.wondershare.com/virtual-location/no-location-found-vs-location-not-available.html)

"No Location Found" means that the Find My app is unable to locate the Nokia C32 device being tracked. This can happen for a variety of reasons, such as if the Nokia C32 device is turned off, not connected to the internet, or if location services are disabled.

"Location Not Available" means that the Nokia C32 device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Nokia C32 device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.
